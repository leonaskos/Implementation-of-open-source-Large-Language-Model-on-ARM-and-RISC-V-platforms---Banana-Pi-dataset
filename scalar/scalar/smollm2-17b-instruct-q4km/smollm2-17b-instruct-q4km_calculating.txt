d(3x^2)/dx + d(5x)/dx - d(7)/dx

- The derivative of 5x is 5 (rule: if the power is 1, the derivative is the coefficient, so the derivative of x is 1).
- The derivative of -7 is 0, because the derivative of any constant is 0.
