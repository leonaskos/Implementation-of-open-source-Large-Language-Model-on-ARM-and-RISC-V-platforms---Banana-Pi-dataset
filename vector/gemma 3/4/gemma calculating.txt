* **Constant Multiple Rule:** The derivative of a constant multiplied by a function is the constant multiplied by the derivative of the function.
* **Sum/Difference Rule:** The derivative of a sum or difference of terms is the sum or difference of the derivatives of the terms.

2. **Derivative of 5x:** 5 * (1x^(1-1)) = 5
3. **Derivative of -7:** The derivative of a constant is 0.

Therefore, the derivative of 3x^2 + 5x - 7 is 6x + 5 + 0 = 6x + 5
