1. **Differentiate \( 3x^2 \):**
   - The coefficient \( 3 \) is a constant.
   - The derivative of \( x^2 \) is \( 2x \).
   - So, \( \frac{d}{dx}(3x^2) = 3 \cdot 2x = 6x \).

2. **Differentiate \( 5x \):**
   - The coefficient \( 5 \) is a constant.
   - The derivative of \( x \) is \( 1 \).
   - So, \( \frac{d}{dx}(5x) = 5 \cdot 1 = 5 \).

3. **Differentiate \( -7 \):**
   - \( \frac{d}{dx}(-7) = 0 \) (since the derivative of a constant is zero).

### Step 3: Combine the results
Now, add the derivatives of all the terms together:

\[
f'(x) = 6x + 5 + 0 = 6x + 5
\]

### Final Answer
\[
