#### Answer:
To find the derivative of the function \( f(x) = 3x^2 + 5x - 7 \), we will apply the power rule to each term. The power rule states that if \( f(x) = x^n \), then \( f'(x) = nx^{n-1} \).

1. **Derivative of \( 3x^2 \):**
   \[
   \frac{d}{dx}(3x^2) = 3 \cdot 2x^{2-1} = 6x
   \]

2. **Derivative of \( 5x \):**
   \[
   \frac{d}{dx}(5x) = 5 \cdot 1x^{1-1} = 5
   \]

3. **Derivative of \( -7 \):**
   \[
   \frac{d}{dx}(-7) = 0
   \]

Combining these results, the derivative of \( f(x) = 3x^2 + 5x - 7 \) is:
\[
f'(x) = 6x + 5
\]

\boxed{6x + 5}
